Recipe for French-Canadian Toast (serves 2)
Ingredients: 2 large eggs; 1 cup milk; 1 teaspoon pure vanilla; ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon; 1 tablespoon icing sugar; 4 thick slices whole wheat bread, lightly toasted; 1 cup thinly sliced fresh fruit with whipped cream and Canadian maple syrup, to taste.
Directions: preheat frying pan to medium • spray pan with nonfat cooking spray • crack eggs into shallow bowl • add milk to eggs and blend briskly with fork • add vanilla and cinnamon, stir in • dip one piece of toast in egg mixture, turning quickly and coating both sides (Warning: do not allow the toast to sit in the mixture for too long, or it will become soggy.) • place toast in frying pan, lightly fry until brown • turn toast over and fry other side • repeat dipping and frying with three other pieces of toast • sprinkle French toast with icing sugar and serve with fresh fruit topping, Canadian maple syrup, and a dollop of whipped cream
